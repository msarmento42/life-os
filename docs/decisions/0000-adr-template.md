# NNNN — Title

**Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Accepted (reconstructed) | Accepted (reconstructed, rationale inferred) | Superseded by NNNN
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What situation made a decision necessary. Constraints in play at the time.

## Decision

The choice that was made, stated plainly, one sentence if possible.

## Consequences

What this makes easier, what it makes harder, what it forecloses.

## Alternatives Rejected

What else was considered and why it lost. If no evidence shows an alternative
was considered, write exactly: `No evidence of alternatives considered.`
Never invent this section — a fabricated alternative is worse than an empty one.

## Evidence

File paths with line ranges, commit SHAs, PR/issue numbers that support this
ADR. An ADR with no Evidence section is invalid. For reconstructed ADRs,
prefix every sentence that is inferred rather than directly stated with
`INFERRED:`.

---

## Amendment rule

**Editorial changes are permitted** — typos, broken links, formatting, adding
evidence citations, clarifying ambiguous wording. These are made in place; the
git history is the audit trail.

**Material changes require a superseding ADR** — anything that alters the
decision, its rationale, its tradeoffs, or its consequences. Mark the original
`Superseded by NNNN` and link forward. Do not edit the original's substance.

The test: *would this edit change what a reader does?* If yes, it is material.
